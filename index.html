Letters to My Sisters 💖

Words I don't say often enough

🧸
The Eldest Kak Je

To Our Dearest Kak Je

To Kak Je, the oldest of us three. Thank you for always being that quiet, comforting presence whenever life gets too loud. Being the oldest isn't always easy, and I know you carry things silently without wanting to burden anyone. But please remember that you don't always have to be the strong one for us. You are allowed to be tired, you are allowed to rest, and you can always lean on us too. Thank you for listening, for guiding us gently, and for making sure we always feel looked after. We love you so much, Kak Je.

Big Sister Safe Haven #01
🎀
The Middle Biyel

To Our Sweet Biyel

To Biyel, the middle bridge that holds our little world together. Thank you for always caring so deeply about the small details and for checking up on us when we go quiet. Being in the middle means holding hands with both the oldest and the youngest, and you do it with so much love and sincerity. Thank you for being the glue that keeps our conversations alive and making sure no one ever feels left out or alone.

Loving Heart #02
🌷
The Youngest Sal

To Our Precious Sal

To our precious youngest sister Sal, you bring so much genuine joy and lightness into our lives just by being you. Growing up and facing the world can feel overwhelming and scary sometimes, but I need you to promise that you will never doubt yourself. Whenever things get tough or you feel lost, remember that you have two older sisters who will always stand behind you, protect you, and cheer for you no matter what. You are so loved, Sal.
